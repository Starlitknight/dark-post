Messages

Ask the GM is private between one player and you. Only you write on the notice board. Broadcasts are what players say to everyone from the broadcast room, and you can broadcast too. You can read every private message, and players are told that you can.

Night 1: Six Vaults

Three vaults are empty and three hold 10, 5 and 2 gold. Each player is dealt one true clue, can buy one more in the shop for 1 coin, and opens two vaults. The least gold is out, and a tie for last is played as Rock Paper Scissors from throws each player seals. Start it after the bags are open. The vaults close by themselves at the time you set, or when you press the button.

Rooms

What players have done in the puzzle room and the betting room. Players never see each other's answers or deals. You see all of it.

Loading.

Use this for prizes, penalties, or the result of a night you ran yourself. It works once the bags are open.

1. Invitations

Each guest gets a private link. Whoever holds a link holds that seat, so send each one to one person only. The name is printed on their letter.

2. Dates

Enter these in your own time. Every guest sees them converted to theirs.

After the bags open, every player's clock in the hall counts down to this. Leave the time empty and the clock says "time to be announced". Change both after each round.

3. The letter

This is the first thing a guest sees. In the last line, {opens} and {reply} become your two dates. A sentence whose date is empty is left out.

4. Opening night

Unticked, players only learn how many took each bag, and their own coins.

5. See what guests see

These previews save nothing. They use your letter and dates.
